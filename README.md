One brutally honest — but still polite — out-of-office auto-reply, in a
single sentence of 190 characters:

> I'm away until Monday, and honestly, most of the emails waiting for me will be deleted unread — thank you for writing anyway, and for your patience with someone who really needed this break.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes that text to `/output/entry.txt`, honouring
`ENTRY_OUT` (file or directory) and `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` first.
